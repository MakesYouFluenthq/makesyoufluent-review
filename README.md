2. How MakesYouFluent Works
MakesYouFluent uses AI-assisted conversations to give learners opportunities to practice speaking in their chosen language.
The general learning process involves selecting a language, choosing a proficiency level, and interacting with an AI tutor through speaking exercises.
Step 1: Select a language
Users choose the language they want to learn from the available courses.
Step 2: Choose a proficiency level
The application allows learners to select a level that reflects their existing ability.
This helps determine the vocabulary, grammar complexity, and conversation difficulty used during practice.
Step 3: Start an AI conversation
The AI tutor introduces conversational exercises and responds to spoken input.
Users can practice discussing everyday subjects, answering questions, and constructing sentences.
Step 4: Receive feedback
The application provides feedback on areas such as pronunciation and grammar.
Automated feedback can help identify mistakes, although speech recognition and AI-generated corrections are not always perfect.
Step 5: Continue practicing
Lessons and conversations provide opportunities to build vocabulary, improve sentence construction, and become more comfortable speaking.
What makes AI language learning different?
Traditional language-learning applications often place considerable emphasis on memorization, vocabulary recognition, and written exercises.
AI conversational applications add another dimension: learners can actively produce language and receive responses within a simulated conversation.
This makes them useful for practicing communication outside a conventional classroom.
However, AI conversations should complement other learning activities, including listening to native speakers, reading, grammar study, and real-world communication.
